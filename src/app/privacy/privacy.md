# Privacy Policy — Animus Project

**Last updated: November 2026**

---

### 1. Sacred Foundation: Radical Privacy & Zero PII

Privacy, individual sovereignty, and human dignity are the non-negotiable reasons the **Animus Project** and its applications exist.

This Privacy Policy governs all applications, websites, and services provided by **Animus Project LLC**, including:
- **Animus Project** (`animusproject.org`)
- **Animus** (`animus.animusproject.org`)
- **Animus Remnant** (`remnant.animusproject.org`)
- **Animus Resonance** (`resonance.animusproject.org`)

**Radical Data Minimization**:
- We do **not** collect, sell, or monetize your personal identity, physical address, telephone number, address book contacts, or social media handles.
- We do **not** collect advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID) or commercial tracking profiles.

---

### 2. Zero-Spyware Commitment & Real-Time Network Auditability

We believe genuine privacy must be provable through technical verification, not blind trust:

- **Absolute Zero-Spyware Guarantee**: None of the applications in the Animus Project suite—across Linux, Windows, Android, iOS, or the web—contain any spyware, adware, commercial tracking SDKs, device fingerprinting libraries, or hidden behavioral telemetry.
- **Strict Network Call Minimization**: Our software **never** initiates network requests or communicates with any server, data broker, or third-party service that is not strictly necessary for the direct execution of features explicitly requested by you.
- **Publicly Auditable**: Any user or independent security researcher can verify our claims by inspecting application network traffic in real time using standard packet inspection tools (such as **Wireshark, Little Snitch, mitmproxy, or Charles Proxy**). You will observe zero background pings to advertising brokers and zero covert analytics beacons.

---

### 3. Technical Session Cookies & Local Storage

- **Strictly Functional Cookies Only**: We use minimal, strictly functional session tokens solely to maintain your authenticated session across authorized subdomains (`animusproject.org`).
- **Zero Third-Party Advertising Cookies**: We never deploy third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or marketing pixels.
- **Local Storage**: Preferences such as interface theme (dark/light mode) and local cryptographic keys are retained directly on your device.

---

### 4. Cryptographic Standards & Security

- **Encryption in Transit**: All network communications between your devices and our servers utilize encrypted TLS 1.3 channels with forward secrecy.
- **Encryption at Rest**: Persistent storage volumes are encrypted using industry-standard AES-256.
- **End-to-End Encrypted Communications**: Private communications and personal vaults are encrypted so that only authorized participants hold access.
- **Zero Third-Party Model Training**: Your personal data, voice samples, and private records are **never** sold, licensed, or used to train public foundation AI models or external commercial algorithms.

---

### 5. Right to Absolute Erasure: Permanent & Irrevocable Deletion

You hold absolute sovereignty over your participation and records:

- **Strict Pre-Deletion Authentication**: Deletion requests require identity verification to prevent accidental loss or unauthorized third-party tampering.
- **Complete, Irrevocable & Permanent Deletion**: Upon confirmed request, our systems execute an irreversible cryptographic purge of all your account records, credentials, and associated data from all active storage clusters.
- **Zero Possibility of Recovery**: We do not perform deceptive "soft-deletes" or retain shadow archives. Once executed, your data is permanently unrecoverable.

Animus complies with international data protection standards:
- **Right to Access & Portability**: You may request and export your account data at any time.
- **Right to Rectification & Erasure**: You may update your account settings or execute irrevocable deletion on demand.
