# Animus — Vision & Manifesto

**Last updated: November 2026**

---

## 1. Vision & Core Purpose

For the past two decades, the internet promised to connect humanity. Instead, it delivered an architecture of division, commercial surveillance, and superficial noise.

**Animus Project exists to build a sovereign alternative:** a new way to see the world, a new way to meet people, and a new way to preserve who you are—built on radical privacy, zero manipulation, and human dignity.

---

## 2. The Three Sovereign Platforms

### I. Animus (`animus.animusproject.org`)
A new way to see the world is coming. Discover what humanity truly thinks in real time—free from social media outrage, manipulation, and algorithmic bias. Architectural and operational details remain confidential until public launch.

### II. Animus Resonance (`resonance.animusproject.org`)
A new way to meet people is coming. Genuine human connection for friendship, collaboration, and relationships—beyond superficial appearances and vanity metrics. Architectural and operational details remain confidential until public launch.

### III. Animus Remnant (`remnant.animusproject.org`)
Your personal AI companion and digital legacy. **Jarvis** assists with your daily computer and mobile tasks while learning how you think, allowing **Remnant** to speak and reason in your authentic voice with complete privacy.

---

## 3. Uncompromising Privacy & Sovereignty

- **Radical Anonymity**: We do not collect or monetize personal identities, contact lists, or behavioral profiles.
- **Zero Spyware & Auditable Network**: Zero tracking SDKs, zero advertising beacons, and zero covert network calls—verifiable at any time via standard network inspection tools.
- **Absolute Ownership & Erasure**: You retain full sovereignty over your data, with permanent and irreversible cryptographic deletion available on demand.
